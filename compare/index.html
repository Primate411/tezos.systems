Where the major proof-of-stake chains stand

A compact read before the head-to-head pages: each chain's profile, what to watch, and how Tezos fits into the field. This is orientation, not a composite score.

Tezos

Self-upgrading baseline

Tezos is the continuity play: deterministic finality, protocol-level self-amendment, adaptive slashing, and no persistent upgrade-driven community split in its tracked history.

Profile
Self-amendment, deterministic finality, on-chain upgrade coordination
Watch
Compare throughput, fees, and concentration only with aligned timestamps and definitions.

Ethereum

Issuance profile

Ethereum combines proof-of-stake checkpoint finality with socially coordinated client releases and hard-fork upgrades.

Profile
Checkpoint finality, socially coordinated upgrades
Watch
Issuance, fees, and independently controlled staking entities are dynamic and method-dependent.

Solana

Speed specialist

Solana emphasizes fast slots and client-coordinated operation under a disinflationary issuance schedule.

Profile
Fast slots, client-coordinated upgrades
Watch
No on-chain self-amendment; review its published incident history separately.

Cardano

Governance entrant

Cardano combines stake-pool consensus with Voltaire-era governance and hard-fork-combinator upgrades.

Profile
Stake pools, newer on-chain governance
Watch
Slower finality and a shorter production record for on-chain upgrades.

Algorand

Finality and efficiency

Algorand uses permissionless sortition and immediate deterministic finality, with Foundation-coordinated upgrades.

Profile
Sortition-based consensus, immediate finality
Watch
Upgrade path is more foundation-coordinated than self-amending.
